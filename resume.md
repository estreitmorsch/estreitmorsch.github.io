## Research Experience

- PhD researcher in the [Grewe Lab](https://grewelab.org/biography/), Institute of Neuroinformatics, ETH Zurich
  - Investigated neural circuit dynamics underlying maternal and offspring-directed behavior, integrating behavioral experiments with multimodal neural and computational analyses.
- Master thesis in [Feil Lab](https://uni-tuebingen.de/en/faculties/faculty-of-science/departments/interfaculty-facilities/ifib/workgroups/groups-a-f/feil/), Interfakultäres Institut für Biochemie, Tübingen, Germany
  - Studied cellular mechanisms relevant to neural development by quantifying primary neuronal growth-cone morphology using confocal microscopy.
- Bachelor thesis in Neuroplasticity Lab - Prof. Roberto Lent, Institute for Biomedicine, Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil
  - Investigated mechanisms of neuronal development and migration in GABAergic and glutamatergic neurons using time-lapse confocal microscopy, establishing a real-time in vitro imaging pipeline.
