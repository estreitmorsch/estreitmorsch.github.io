A story-teller accostumed to bridging worlds, people and places.

I am Eduarda (also known as Duda), born and raised in the _Cidade Maravilhosa_ of Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Currently I live in _Zuri-City_, finishing my PhD under the supervision of [Prof. Benjamin Grewe](https://grewelab.org/biography/) at the Institute of Neuroinformatics, ETH Zurich. My PhD research is on Social and Behavioral Neuroscience (more on that later on the blog).

My academic journey took me from Rio to Marseille, France for an exchange program, and then to Tubingen, Germany for my Masters, and now I am finally in Zurich, Switzerland; always through my passion for Neuroscience, and my search to understand better how we come to behave and to move through life as coffee-drinking and tax-paying primates. Growing up with a pilot for a dad and a therapist for a mom, I find comfort on the move between countries and cultures, and I crave the understanding behind motivation and action of people.

At heart, I am a bookworm, which made me create my own bookclub and be an active participant in bookclubs around Zurich. As famously Dr. Seuss said,

>“The more that you read, the more things you will know. The more that you learn, the more places you’ll go.” – Dr. Seuss 

Literature and art are two of the guides of my life: I feel the most of _myself_ when I am enveloped with them. And I'll make sure there's lots of these in this space here.
